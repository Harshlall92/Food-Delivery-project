# Part 2 — Team Agreement

Your Team Agreement should clearly describe **how your team will work together**.

Address each of the following:

### 1\. Team Values

What values will guide your team?  
For example: respect, accountability, communication, reliability, inclusion, and fairness.

Our team will be guided by respect, accountability, communication, reliability, inclusion, and fairness. We agree to treat each other's time and contributions as equally valuable, to be honest about our progress and limitations, and to support one another in completing our shared work to a high standard. 

### 2\. Meetings

* How often will you meet?  
  	We will meet twice a week; every Monday and Friday 5pm \-8pm.  

* When and where will you meet?  
  	We will book a meeting room in commons the week before, and hold the meetings there.

* What happens if someone cannot attend?
    If someone does not attend the meeting, they must try to inform at least a day earlier so the meeting can be rescheduled in the same week, else catch up from meeting minutes. 

### 3\. Communication

* What communication tools will you use?  
  	We will be using WhatsApp to primarily communicate with each other by either texting in the group chat or just calling with everyone.  

* Which tool will be used for urgent matters?  
  	When things get urgent, we will just call each other to sort it out.
 
* How will important information be shared with the entire team?  
  	It will either be on group chat (mostly) or github(for files).

### 4\. Response Expectations

* How quickly should team members respond to messages?
    Team members must respond within 24 hours on weekdays.

* What should someone do if they cannot respond?
	They should let the team know when their next availability is as soon as possible.

* What happens if a member repeatedly fails to respond?
	If a member repeatedly fails to respond more than twice without explanation, the team will first reach out directly to check in, and if the issue persists, it will be raised to the TA or the instructor.

### 5\. Respect & Professional Conduct

* What does respectful behavior mean to your team?  
  	We believe respectful behavior means that we will talk to each other with respect and be understanding of others not having availability as this is not the only project they have.

* What behaviors are unacceptable?
    Some behaviors that would be unacceptable would be disrespecting other team members for their mistakes on the project and being disrespectful in general should be unacceptable.

* How will you make sure everyone has an opportunity to participate?  
  	We will make sure everyone can participate by planning and distributing the work and also allowing others to help if they get stuck.


### 6\. Accountability

What will your team do when a member does not meet the expectations agreed upon by the team?

	As mentioned above, we will try to communicate this issue with them at least twice, then physically meet with them if not already and if the issue still persists, we will talk to the TA or the instructor.

### 7\. Decision-Making

How will your team make decisions?

* How decisions will be made  
  	Most decisions will be made by discussion and majority vote, since our team is small.  
* Whether you will use consensus, voting, or another method  
  	We will be doing a majority vote.  
* What happens when team members disagree  
  	When the members disagree, we will be weighing pros and cons together upon hearing all the viewpoints, and move forward with a solution that serves the project and its deadlines best.

### 8\. Conflict Resolution

How will your team handle disagreements?

	Step 1 \- Direct conversation : the team members will raise a disagreement and try to solve it by discussing it further.

	Step 2 \- If two members cannot fix the issue by themselves the other team members will step in for a group discussion. 

	Step 3 \- If the issue still remains, we might escalate it to the TA.

Describe the steps you will take to resolve conflicts within the team before involving the instructor or TA.

	The individuals will first try to resolve the disagreements directly and respectfully, if unresolved, the full team will discuss the issue together to find a fair solution before escalating it to the TA.

### 9\. Anticipated Challenges

Identify potential challenges your team may face and explain how you will address them.

* Missed meetings \- Notify the team in advance, missed information will be shared via meeting notes or by calling the teammate at a later point before the next meeting.  
    
* Missed deadlines \- We would build in buffer times and check in on each team member’s progress before the deadlines.

* Unequal contributions \- We will assign clear trackable tasks and check in regularly to catch imbalances.  
    
* Poor communication \- each team member must stick to the agreed response times and communication channels.

* Scheduling conflicts \- Already solved as the entire team agreed on a meeting time for the duration of the project.  
    
* Disagreements between team members \- Follow the conflict resolution steps outlined above.
